Your account is personal and can belong to many workspaces. Go to **Settings → Personal** to manage it.

## Sign in

Andesine has no passwords. Sign in with one of these:

- **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**.
- **Email:** Andesine sends a one-time code to your email address.
- **Passkey:** sign in with one click, using your device's biometrics or a hardware security key.

Add a passkey after your first sign-in to sign in faster.

## Passkeys

Go to **Settings → Personal → Security → Passkeys** and select **Add a passkey**. Follow the steps of your browser or device. You can **Rename** a passkey, for example `Work laptop`, or **Delete** it.

## Profile

In **Settings → Personal → Profile**, set your **Full name** and profile image. Your name shows in all your workspaces.

## Workspaces and accounts

Open the profile menu, which shows your name and image:

- **Switch workspace** opens another workspace that you are a member of.
- **Create workspace** creates a new workspace, with you as its admin.
- **Add account** signs in with another account, such as a work account, without signing out. Switch between accounts in the same menu.
- **Log out** signs out of the current account.

## Connected apps

**Settings → Personal → Connected apps** lists the CLI, MCP clients, and other apps that you connected to your account, and the workspace that each one can access. Select **Revoke access** to disconnect an app at once. See [Connect an AI app](/docs/ai-agents/connect-an-ai-app/).

## Delete your account

Go to **Settings → Personal → Danger zone** and select **Delete account**.

- Workspaces where you are the only admin are deleted for all their members, and their subscriptions are cancelled. To keep a workspace, make another member an admin first.
- In other workspaces, your membership is removed. The content that you wrote stays, without your name.
- Your profile, sign-in methods, and connected apps are deleted.

This cannot be undone.

## Delete a workspace

Go to **Settings → General → Danger zone** and select **Delete workspace**. All content, versions, images, and settings of the workspace are deleted at once, and the subscription is cancelled. This cannot be undone. You need the **Workspace** permission.

To keep a copy of your content first, use [GitHub Sync](/docs/publishing/github-sync/), the [CLI](/docs/developers/cli/), or the [API](/docs/developers/api-overview/).
